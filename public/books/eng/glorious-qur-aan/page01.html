Part One

Virtues of the Glorious Qur-aan

It is necessary for the reader of the Qur-aan to respect of the Qur-aan. One who has no respect is deprived of Allah's special favour.

It is said that whenever Hadrat Ikramah (Radhi Allaho anho) opened the Qur-aan for recitation, he would become unconscious and fall down. Then he would say:

"This is the Word of my Allah, this is the Word of my Allah."
