Rules of External Respect

  • 1.    Perform wudhu and then sit respectfully facing Qiblah.

  • 2.    Do not read fast, rather read at a medium speed with correct pronunciation.

  • 3.    Try to weep.

  • 4.    When reading the aayaat of mercy or of punishment, do as explained above.

  • 5.    Read in a loud voice. However, if others will get disturbed by your recitation, then read softly.

  • 6.    Read in a sweet voice.
