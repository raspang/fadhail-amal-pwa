Hadith No -13

Hadrat Ali (Radhi Allaho anho) says that Rasulullah (Sallallaho alayhi wasallam) said, "Whoever reads the Qur-aan and learns it by heart, and regards what it makes halaal as halaal and its haraam as haraam, will be entered into Jannah by Allah Ta’ala who will also accept his begging forgiveness for ten such persons of his family who were supposed to go to Jahannum."

By the grace of Allah Ta’ala, entry into Jannah is promised for every Muslim though it may come after him being punished for his sins. The Hafiz, however, will be allowed to go straight to Jannah. The ten persons who will be forgiven will be those sinful and disobedient Muslims who are guilty of major sins. There will be no forgiveness, however, for non-muslims.
