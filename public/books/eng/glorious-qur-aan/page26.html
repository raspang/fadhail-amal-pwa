Hadith No -22

Hadrat Abu Hurairah (Radhi Allaho anho) says that Rasulullah (Sallallaho alayhi wasallam) said, "Never do a people gather in one of the houses (musjids) of Allah Ta’ala reciting the Qur-aan and reading it out to one another, but Sakinah (peace) comes down on them, Rahmat (mercy) covers them, the angels sit around them and Almighty Allah mentions them, in the gathering of the angels."

Sakinah means peace of heart.

A story about Usaid ibn Hudhair (Radhi Allaho anho) is given in the books of hadith. It is said that while he was reading the Qur-aan, he felt a sort of cloud spread over him. Nabi (sallallahu alayhi wasallam) informed him that these were angels who had gathered to listen to the reading of the Qur-aan. Due to their large number, they appeared like a cloud.
