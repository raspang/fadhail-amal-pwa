Hadith No -38

Hadrat Abu Hurairah (Radhi Allaho anho) reports that Rasulullah (Sallallaho alayhi wasallam) said, "Whoever reads ten aayaat at night, will not be counted amongst the neglectful."

It takes only a few minutes to read ten aayaat. Doing so saves a person from being included in the list of the neglectful, for that night. It is really a great reward.
