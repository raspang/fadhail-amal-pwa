Hadith No -20

Hadrat A'ishah (Radhi Allaho anha) says that Rasulullah (Sallallaho alayhi wasallam) said, Hadrat A'ishah (Radhi Allaho anha) says that Rasulullah (Sallallaho alayhi wasallam) said, "Certainly there is always a thing in which people take pride. The glory and pride for my Ummat is the Qur-aan."

Now, we should think about our own condition. How many of us feel really proud of having memorized the Qur-aan? Does a hafiz earn real respect in our eyes? Alas! Our honour and pride is in high university degrees, in big titles, in worldly boasts and show, and in the wealth, which we will have to leave behind on our death.

O, Allah! Have mercy on us.
