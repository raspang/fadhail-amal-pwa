Chapter Seven

31. Shu’bah, a Muhaddith, gives a beam of his roof to a supplicant

Shu'bah Rahmatullah alaihe was a renowned Muhaddith who was called Ameer-ul-Mo’mineen-fil-Hadith (The Commander of the Faithful in the realm of Hadith), and was also known for his devoted worship and asceticism. Once a certain man came to ask a favour of him. The Muhaddith had nothing to give and, therefore, he removed a beam from the ceiling of his room and gave it to the man, saying, "Take it and sell it in the market I am terribly sorry, for I have nothing else to give you, at the moment.” (It-Haaf)
