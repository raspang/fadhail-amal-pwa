Chapter Seven

32. Abu Sahl Su’luki gives his water jug to a supplicant

Sheikh Abu Sahl Su'luki Rahmatullah alaihe was once performing Wudhu (ablution) when a person came and asked a favour of him. The Sheikh had nothing to give and, so, he asked the man to wait a while. When he had finished his Wudhu, he gave the man the wooden jug which he was using for Wudhu saying; "Sorry, I have nothing else to give you." (It-haaf)
