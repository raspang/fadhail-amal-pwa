Chapter Seven

34. Bishr bin Haafi gives away his upper garment on his death-bed

Abbas bin Dihqaan Rahmatullah alaihe says that he has never known anyone who departed from the world empty-handed and denuded of everything, as he was born, except for Sheikh Bishr bin Haafi Rahmatullah alaihe. When his hour of death drew near for him, a certain man came and asked him for help in his need. The Sheikh, lying in bed, stripped the upper garment from his body and gave it to him. He borrowed a garment from one of his friends, which he wore till his death. (It-haaf)
