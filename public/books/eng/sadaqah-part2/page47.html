Chapter Seven

21. Qais bin Sa’d Khazraji cancels the debts of his friends and makes a proclamation

Once Qais Bin Sa‘ad Khazraji Rahmatullah alaihe fell ill, but few of his friends came to enquire about his health. He was amazed, especially when he missed those who frequented his house when he had been in health. He asked about it from his people and they said, "Almost everybody owes you something and they feel embarrassed to visit you without paying what they owe". At this, Qais Rahmatullah alaihe said, "Cursed be this wealth! It deprives a man of the pleasure of meeting his friends". He then asked someone to proclaim all around the city that Qais had absolved all his debtors from paying their debts. After the proclamation, the visitors came in such large numbers that there was always a crowd in the house. (It-haaf)
