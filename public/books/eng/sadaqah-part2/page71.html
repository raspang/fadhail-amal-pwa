Chapter Seven

45. Junaid returns a gift sent for his students and disciples

Once, a certain man presented a gift of five hundred Dirhams to Sheikh Junaid Rahmatullah alaihe of Baghdad and requested that the amount should be distributed among the Sheikh’s students and disciples. The Sheikh said to him, "Do you possess any more Dirhams?” The man said that he had a lot of Dinaars in his possession. The Sheikh said, "Do you wish for increase in your wealth or, are you content with what you possess?" The man said that he desired an increase in his property. The Sheikh said,“Then, your need is greater than ours, for, we do not wish for an increase in what we possess in the world". Saying this, the Sheikh declined to accept the gift and gave the money back to him. (Raudh)
