Chapter Seven

20. Instances of the generosity of Sa’eed bin Khalid Umavi

Sa'eed Bin Khalid Umawi Rahmatullah alaihe was a very rich man, widely known for his wealth and prosperity. It was his habit that, whenever a needy person came to him for help, he would most generously give him out of what he possessed. And, if sometimes a person asked him for help when he did not possess anything, he would make a committment in writing for the man to approach him when he was in better circumstances or ask his heirs after his death. (It-haaf)
