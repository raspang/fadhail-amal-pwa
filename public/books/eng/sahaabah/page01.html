Part One

Steadfastness in the face of Hardships

It is really very hard for the Muslims of today to imagine and much less to bear, or even attempt to bear the hardships that were experienced by Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) and his illustrious companions in the path of Allah Ta’ala. Books of history are full of stories of their sufferings. It is a pity that we are so indifferent to those events and our knowledge is so poor in this regard. I open this chapter with a story about Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) himself, whose name is sure to attract the blessings of Allah Ta’ala.
