Part Four

Piety and Scrupulousness

The habits and character of the Sahaabah (RA), as a whole, are worth following, as they were the people specially chosen and selected by Allah Ta’ala to be the companions of His beloved Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam).

Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) says: "I have been sent in the best period of human history."

The time of Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) was itself a blessed period, and the people favoured with his company were really the cream of that age.
