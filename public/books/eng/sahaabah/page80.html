Part Twelve

Different Stories about Sahaabah's (RA) Love for Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam)
Story 1

'Umar (RA) once said to Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam): "O Nabi of Allah, you are dearer to me than anybody else in the world except my own self."

Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) replied: "Nobody can be a perfect Mo’min until I am dearer to him than even his own self."

Umar (RA) then said: "Now you are dearer to me than my own self."

Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) replied: "Now, O’ Umar."

The 'Ulama have given two meanings to the last words of Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) namely:

  • 1.    "Now you have real Imaan."

  • 2.    "Why is it that it is only now that I am dearer to you than your own self? This should have been so long ago."

Suhail Tastari (Rahmatullah alaih) says: "No one can have enjoyment of the Sunnat until he takes Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) as his Master and considers himself as Nabi (Sallallahu Alayhi Wasallam) slave."
