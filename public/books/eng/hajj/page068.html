Chapter 8: Ziyyaarat of Madinah (Visiting the Grave of Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam))

Hadith Number Seven

Hazrat Anass (RA) reports that Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) said: 'who ever visits me in Madinah with the object of gaining spiritual reward, shall be in my company and for him shall I be an intercessor on the day of Qiyaamat.' (Baihaqi)

We have already mentioned the contents of this Hadith under Hadith number four. Some people read the word 'Jawaari' with a dhamma, so that it now means that visitors to Nabi (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) shall be in his security; promise of safety and protection. What greater fortune can there be then this?'
