Conclusion - The Farewell Hajj of Rasulullah (Salallaho Alaihi Wassallam)

Story No. 7: (Episode)

When Hazrat Fudhail bin Iyaadh (RA) was on the plain of Arafaat he too remained silent and only when the sun had set did he say: "O Allah, even though You may have forgiven me, still I bewail the wretched of my condition." (Rowdh)
