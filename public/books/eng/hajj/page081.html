Chapter 9: The Aadaab for the Ziyaarat (The Manner of Performing The Ziyaarat)

Story No. 7: (Episode)

Hazrat Ibrahim Khawaas Rah relates: "Once on a journey I suffered such thirst that while traveling I fell unconscious. While in that state someone threw water on my face. When I regained my senses I beheld a very handsome man on horseback. He gave me water to drink and bade me to join him on his horse. I accepted his offer. Having gone a short distance he turned to me asking: "What place is this?" I replied: "This is the enlightened city of Madinah." He said: Descend now and when you reach the holy grave of Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) say to him that your brother Khidr sends his choicest salaams and greetings. (Rowda)
