Chapter 9: The Aadaab for the Ziyaarat (The Manner of Performing The Ziyaarat)

Story No. 32: (Episode)

Abdah the daughter of Khalid bin Hazrat Ma'dan (RA) says: "Whenever my father used to lie down to sleep at night he used to become very restless and impatient to see Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam). Then he used to call out the names of the Hazrat Muhajireen (RA) and Hazrat Ansar (RA) and say: "O Allah these men are my predecessors and indeed does my heart long to meet them and my longing and yearning for them knows no bounds. O Allah let death overtake me quickly that I may meet them. " This he used to say until he fell asleep." (shifa)
