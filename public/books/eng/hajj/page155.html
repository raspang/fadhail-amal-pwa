Conclusion - The Farewell Hajj of Rasulullah (Salallaho Alaihi Wassallam)

Story No. 39: (Episode)

Hazrat Ibrahim Khawaas (RA)says: "While traveling in the wilderness once, I experienced great hardships and many misfortunes. However I patiently persevered and came through. When I entered Makkah a certain feeling of pride came into me and that too while I was performing tawaaf. From behind me an elderly lady called out to me: "O Ibrahim! This servant of Allah was with you in the desert (wilderness), but I did not want to disturb you by talking to you, because I did not want to take your attention from Allah. Take out that vanity which now has entered your heart." (Rowdh)
