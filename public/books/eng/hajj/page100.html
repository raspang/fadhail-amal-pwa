Chapter 9: The Aadaab for the Ziyaarat (The Manner of Performing The Ziyaarat)

Story No. 26: (Episode)

Hazrat Thabit bin Ahmad Abul Qasim Baghdadi (RA) relates that he once saw a muazzin reciting the azaan for Fair salaah in the Masjid of Madinah. When the muazzin said:

"Assalaato Khairum minan nowm."

(Verily salaah is better than sleep).

A servant came along and struck him a hard smack. Crying he exclaimed. "O Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) see what is done to me in your esteemed presence!" immediately after the complaint the servant was paralyzed and fell down. People who were nearby picked him up and took his home where after three days he died. (Wafaa)
