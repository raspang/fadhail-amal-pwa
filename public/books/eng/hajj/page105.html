Chapter 9: The Aadaab for the Ziyaarat (The Manner of Performing The Ziyaarat)

Story No. 31: (Episode)

A lady came to hazrat Aa'isha (RA) begging her to let her see the grave of Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) in the room where he is buried. Hazrat Aa'isha (RA) pulled away the curtain and when the lady saw the grave she cried so bitterly and continuously that she died there. (shifa)
