Chapter 9: The Aadaab for the Ziyaarat (The Manner of Performing The Ziyaarat)

Story No. 15: (Episode)

Hazrat Shaikh Nasr Abdul Wahid bin Abdul Malik bin Muhammad bin Abi Saeed Soofi al kar-khi Rah says: "After Haj I went to visit Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) in Madinah. Having greeted him I was seated near the grave when shaikh Abu Bakr Diyar Bakri arrived and greeted Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam): "Assalamu Alayka ya Rasulullah."

From inside the room of the grave I heard a voice replying: "Wa Alaykumsalaam ya Aba Bakr." All those in the immediate vicinity also heard this reply.
