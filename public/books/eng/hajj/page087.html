Chapter 9: The Aadaab for the Ziyaarat (The Manner of Performing The Ziyaarat)

Story No. 13: (Episode)

Hazrat Sayyed Ahmad Rifa'ee Rah is very well known as one of the foremost saints and mystics of Islaam. In the year 555 A.H. he went for Haj. Thereafter he visited Madinah and standing in front of the grave of Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) he recited the following couplets:

"From far off the thee did I send my soul,

On my behalf to greet you in your resting place,

Here now O Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) is my body to greet you,

Stretch forth your hand that my lips may kiss you."

Indeed was the hand of Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) stretched from the grave and in the presence of an estimated 90 thousand visitors did Sayyed Ahmad kiss it. (See Al Haawee by Suyuti)
