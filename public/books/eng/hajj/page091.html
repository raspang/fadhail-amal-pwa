Chapter 9: The Aadaab for the Ziyaarat (The Manner of Performing The Ziyaarat)

Story No. 17: (Episode)

Hazrat Ali (RA) relates: "Shortly after the burial of Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) a bedouin came to the grave and said: "O Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) we have indeed heard all that you came to convey and whatever Allah had revealed to you did you preserve and so did we. Indeed in that book, which Allah revealed to you do I find this verse?

"And when they were unjust to themselves, they come unto thee and ask Allah's forgiveness; and the Rasul had asked forgiveness for them; they would have found Allah indeed Oft-Forgiving, Most Merciful. (Surah Nisaa-64)
