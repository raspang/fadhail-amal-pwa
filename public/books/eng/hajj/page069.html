Chapter 8: Ziyyaarat of Madinah (Visiting the Grave of Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam))

Hadith Number Eight

Hazrat Abbaas (RA) reports that Rasulullah (Sallaho Alaihe Wassallam) said: ' Who ever performs his Haj in Makkah, then comes to Madinah with the sole aim of visiting me in my mosque, for him shall be written the rewards of two accepted Hajs.' (Dailami-Ithaaf)

Two Hajs here means the reward for his Haj will be double.
