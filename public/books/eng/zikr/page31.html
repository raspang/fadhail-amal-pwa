Hadith No -11

Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) has said, "Those who believe in Laailaaha illallaah will neither have fear in the grave nor on the Day of Qiyaamah. It is as if I see the scene when they will rise from their graves, wiping dust from their heads and saying: 'All praise is for Allah Ta’ala who has removed all worry and fear from us." It is said in another hadith that those who believe in Laailaaha illallaah will experience no problems at the time of death or in the grave.

According to one hadith, a person who recites Laailaaha illallaah one hundred times daily will on the Day of Qiyaamah be raised up with his face shining like the full moon.
