Hadith No -2

Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) is reported to have said: "Once Hazrat Moosa (Alayhis salaam) made dua to Allah Ta’ala requesting to teach him some zikr for His remembrance. He was advised to recite Laailaaha illallaah. He said: 'O my Lord! This zikr is read by all the creation. Again came the reply: "Recite Laailaaha illallaah." He again said: 'O my Sustainer, I want something special, only for me." Allah Ta’ala replied: 'O Musa! If the seven heavens and the seven earths were placed in one pan of the scale and the Kalimah Laailaaha illallaah in the other, the Kalimah will weigh more."
