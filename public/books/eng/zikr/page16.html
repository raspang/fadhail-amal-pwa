Hadith No -15

Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) said, "Practice Zikr so much that people may regard you as mad." It is stated in another Hadith, "Practice Zikr so much that the hypocrite may regard you as insincere."

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbaas (Radi Allahu anhu) says: "Nothing has been made Fardh by Allah Ta’ala without fixing a maximum limit for it except His Zikr, for which no limit has been fixed and no person, as long as he is sane is free from it." Allah Ta’ala has ordered in the Holy Quran; "Practice Zikr of Allah a lot." A person should do Zikr under all conditions, whether by day or night; whether in the jungle or at sea; whether travelling or at home; whether he is rich or poor; whether in sickness or health; whether loudly or quietly.

Hazrat Jaabir (Radi Allahu anhu) says that Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) had once said, "Hazrat Jibraeel (Alayhis Salam) stressed so much on doing Zikr that I felt that without Zikr nothing can help me at all."
