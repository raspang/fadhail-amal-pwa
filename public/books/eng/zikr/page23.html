Hadith No -3

Hadhrat Abu Hurairah (Radhiyallaho anhu) once asked Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) as to who would be most benefitted by his intercession on the Day of Qiyaamah. Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) replied, "Knowing your thirst for the knowledge of hadith, I could expect that none other than you would have asked this question." Thereafter Nabi (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) told Abu Hurairah (Radhiyallaho anhu) that the most blessed or the most benefitted by his intercession will be the person who reads Laailaaha illallaah with sincerity.
