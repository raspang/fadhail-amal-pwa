Hadith No -2

Hadhrat Abu Zar (Radhiyallaho anhu) narrated that once Rasulullah (Sallallaho alayhi wasallam) had said, "Should I tell you what speech is most liked by Allah Ta’ala?" "Do tell me", I replied. He said "It is subhaanallaahi wabihamdihi." Another hadith relates, "The thing that Allah Ta’ala ordered His angels to recite is definitely the best one, and it is subhaanallaahi wabihamdihi.”
