Story -3

Imaam Abu Haneefa (Rahmatullah alaih) is famous for his ibaadat. It is said that for thirty, forty or fifty years (according to the information of different people) he read his Fajr Salaah with the wudhu for Ishaa (meaning that he did not sleep for the entire night). He would go to sleep only for a few minutes in the afternoon saying, "It is sunnat to sleep in the afternoon."
