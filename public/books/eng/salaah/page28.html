Warning on giving up Salaah with Jamaat
Hadith -1

Rasulullah (Sallallaho alayhi wasallam) said, "If a person in spite of hearing the azaan does not go to the masjid (and he prefers to say his salaah at home) without a strong excuse, then his salaah is not accepted. When the Companions inquired as to what could be a strong excuse, he replied, "Illness or fear."

In another hadith, it is stated that such a person is guilty of disobedience to Allah Ta’ala and His Prophet (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam). Hadhrat Ibn Abbaas (Radi Allahu anhu) also says, "No good is done by, nor any good is done to the person who does not join jamaat after hearing the Azaan. Hadhrat Abu Hurairah (Radi Allahu anhu) says, "It is better to pour molten-lead into the ears of a person who does not go to join jamaat."
