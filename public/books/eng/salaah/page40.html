Story -8

Muhammad bin Munkadir (Rahmatullah alaih) was a Hafiz of Hadith (a person who has at least mentioned 100 000 ahaadith). One night, he cried bitterly in Tahajjud. When someone asked about it, he said, "While reading, I came across the following words of the Qur-aan: "And the evils that they earned will confront them; and they will be surrounded by what they used to mock at." He was very worried at the time of his death, and said that these same words of the Qur-aan were troubling him.
