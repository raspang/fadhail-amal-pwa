Hadith -4

Hadhrat Anas bin Maalik (Radi Allahu anhu) narrates that he heard Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) saying, "A person who is sincerely particular in his salaah with Jamaat for forty days, without missing the first takbeer, receives two certificates: one for being saved from Jahannam and the other for freedom from nifaaq (being a munaafiq)."

If a person is regular in his salaah (with sincerity) for forty days and joins the Jamaat from the very start (i.e., when the Imaam calls out his first takbeer), then he shall neither be a munaafiq nor shall he go to Jahannam. A munaafiq is a person who pretends to be a Muslim, but there is kufr (disbelief) in his heart. Lucky are the persons who do not miss their first takbeer for years together.
