Hadith -4 (a)

Hadhrat Abu Hurairah (Radi Allahu anhu) narrates that once Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) asked his companions, 'Do you believe that dirt can remain on a person bathing five times a day in a stream running in front of his door?' 'No', replied the companions, 'No dirt can remain on his body.' Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) said: So, exactly similar is the effect of salaah offered five times a day. With the Grace of Allah Ta’ala, it washes away all the sins'."
