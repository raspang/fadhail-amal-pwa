The Salaah of some Pious People
Story -1

Imaam Ahmad bin Hambal (Rahmatullah alaih) is one of the famous Imaams. Besides being busy in his usual work, he used to read daily three hundred rakaats of nafl salaah. After he was lashed by the king for refusing to listen to him, he became very weak and reduced his nafl salaah to one hundred and fifty rakaats. We should not forget that he was eighty years old at that time.
