Story -6

Abu 'Aamir (Rahmatullah alaih) says, "I saw a slave woman on sale for a very small price. She was very thin and her hair was dirty. I took pity on her and bought her. I said to her, 'Come, woman, let us go and buy things for Ramadhaan.' She said, 'Alhamdulillah, all the months are alike for me.' She fasted on all days and stood in salaah for all nights. When Eid came near, I said to her, 'Woman! You will go with me tomorrow to buy things for Eid. She said, 'My master! You love this world too much.' She then went into her room and started her salaah. She was reciting Soorah Ibrahim and when she reached the 16th verse of the Soorah (viz., Jahannam is before him and he is made to drink boiling, stinking water', which described the punishment of a disbeliever, she repeated it again and again, and then gave out a cry and fell dead."
