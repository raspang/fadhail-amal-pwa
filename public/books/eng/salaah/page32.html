Hadith -5

Somebody asked Ibn Abbaas (Radi Allahu anhu), "What about a person who keeps fast all day and offers nafl salaah all night, but does not go to the masjid for jamaat and Jumu'ah?" "He is doomed to Jahannam", replied Hadhrat Ibn Abbaas (Radi Allahu anhu). Such a person, being a Muslim, may finally come out of Jahannam, but who knows after how long. It is stated in a hadith that Allah Ta’ala curses three persons: An Imaam who insists on leading the people in salaah, although they do not like him for a good reason, a woman whose husband is angry with her; and a person who hears the Azaan but does not go to the masjid for salaah with Jamaat.
