Part Five - Stories about sending Durood and Salaam

Story 1

Umar bin Abdul Azīz (may Allah have mercy on him) was a famous Taabi’ee (one who saw the sahaabah) and a rightly-guided Leader. He would send a special messenger from Syria to Madīna to give his salaams at the grave of Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wassalam).
