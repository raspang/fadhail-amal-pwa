Story 5

A Shaykh was in need of water for wuzu whilst traveling but had no rope or bucket [to pull up the water with from the well]. A girl who was watching came and asked the Shaykh what was the problem. He informed her after which she spat in the well. As a result, the water rose to the top of the well. The Shaykh was shocked after seeing this and asked how she had done this. The girl replied that this was the blessing of sending a great amount of durood and salaams.
