Story 8

Hafiz Ibn Ḥajar Makkī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written that a certain pious person had made a habit of sending a fixed number of durood at bedtime. He saw in a dream one night that the Messenger of Allah (sallallahu alayhi wasallam) came to his house, causing the whole house to become bright. Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi Wassalam) said, "Bring forward that mouth which sends durood and salaams upon me, so that I may kiss it." Because of shyness he put his cheek forward. Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wassalam) kissed his cheek. He then awoke to find that his house was filled with the fragrance of musk.
