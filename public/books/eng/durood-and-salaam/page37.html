Story 15

Abu Qaasim Marwazī (may Allah have mercy on him) says, "My father and I would sit at night to study ḥadīth. It was seen in a dream that a pillar of light is extending from the place we used to sit in and study all the way to the heavens. Someone asked, 'What is this pillar of light?' The reply was, 'These are the blessings they sent when sitting to study the ahaadīth.'
