Story 10

When Abu Abbaas Aḥmad bin Manṣūr (may Allah have mercy on him) passed away, someone saw in a dream that he was standing in the front part (miḥraab) of the main masjid of the city, wearing a beautiful kurta and a crown full of jewels and pearls. The person seeing the dream asked him about this. He replied, "Allah Ta’ala forgave me and treated me with great respect. He gave me this crown. This was all in reward for sending durood and salaam upon Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wassalam) abundantly."
