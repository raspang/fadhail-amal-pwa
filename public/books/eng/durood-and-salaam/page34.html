Story 12

A saint once saw an ugly person/figure in his dream. He asked, “Who are you?” It replied, ‘I am your evil deeds.’ The saint asked, “How can I remain safe from you?” It replied, ‘Send durood and salaams abundantly upon Muhammad (sallallahu alayhi wasallam) the Chosen One.”

Who is there amongst us who is not involved in sins day and night? Sending durood and salaams is the best way of forgiveness. We should send durood upon him as much as possible at all times and not lag behind for this is the greatest medicine.
