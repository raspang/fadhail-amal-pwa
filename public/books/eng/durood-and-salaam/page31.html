Story 9

When Hadhrat Ḥawwaa (RA) was created, Sayyidunaa Aadam (alayhis salaam) moved towards her. The angels said, "Be patient until you are married and paid the dowry." He asked, "What is the dowry?" The angels replied, "To send durood and salaams upon Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wassalam) three times."
