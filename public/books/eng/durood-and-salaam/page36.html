Story 14

Ibn abi Sulaymaan (RA) says, “I saw my father in a dream after his death. I asked, ‘How did Allah Ta’ala deal with you?’ He replied, ‘Allah Ta’ala forgave me.’ I asked, ‘Due to which action did He forgive you?’ He answered, ‘I used to write durood after Rasulullah’s (sallallahu alayhi wasallam) name in every hadith.’”
