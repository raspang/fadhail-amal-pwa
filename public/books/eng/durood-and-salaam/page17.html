Hadith No -2

Hadhrat Alī (RA) narrates that Nabi (sallallahu alayhi wasallam) said, "The miser is he in whose presence I am mentioned and he fails to send durood upon me." Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wasallam) said, "It is enough for the miserliness of a person (i.e. to call a person a miser) that he does not send durood upon me when I am mentioned in his presence."

Hadhrat Abu Zar Ghifaarī (RA) narrates, "I was once present in the gathering of Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wassalam). Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wassalam) asked the Sahaabah, "Shall I not inform you of the stingiest amongst people?" The Sahaabah replied, "Yes, indeed." Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wassalam) said, "The person in whose presence my name is mentioned and does not send durood upon me is the stingiest of all people."
