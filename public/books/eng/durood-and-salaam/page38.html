Story 16

Ḥasan bin Mūsa Ḥaḍramī (may Allah have mercy on him), commonly known as Ibn Ujaynah, says, "I used to write ḥadīths but would leave out the writing of durood to save time. I saw Rasulullah (Sallallahu alayhi wassalam) in my dream. He said to me, 'Why do you not write durood when you write ḥadīths in the same way Abu Amr Ṭabarī does?' When I woke up, I was extremely worried. From that point onwards, I decided to always write (sallallahu alayhi wasallam) when writing aḥaadīth."
