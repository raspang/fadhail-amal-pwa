Story 11

A pious person says, "In my dream, I saw a man named Misṭaḥ who had passed away and who had been a very sinful person. I asked him, 'How did Allah Ta’ala treat you?' He said, 'Allah Ta’ala forgave me.' I asked, 'Why?' He said, 'I was narrating aḥaadith in the presence of a ḥadīth scholar (muḥaddith), who sent durood and I too sent durood very loudly. On hearing me, everyone present in the gathering also sent durood. Allah Ta’ala forgave everyone present in the gathering.'"
